          Frequently Asked Questions

          Which languages are used for teaching?

          The HackStark Ethical Hacking Course for Beginners is taught in Urdu, Hindi and English. TechFly CyberStart Level 1 and the Oxege professional program are taught in Urdu and Hindi.

          How do I enroll and receive access?

          For the self-paced course, create an account or sign in, choose a payment method, complete payment and send your receipt on WhatsApp with your account email. Access starts after manual verification and confirmed approval. Ask about the expected verification time before paying. Partner-program enrollment and active batches are confirmed directly.

          Can international students pay?

          Yes. PayPal, Binance Pay, USDT, Bitcoin (BTC) and other crypto by arrangement are accepted. Contact us on WhatsApp before paying to confirm recipient details, the final amount, currency and any fees. For crypto, confirm both the asset and network. INR prices are reference prices; confirm settlement details first.

          How long is access, and what support is included?

          Ask HackStark to confirm the access period and support arrangements before paying. For instructor-led programs, also confirm the active batch, schedule and certificate terms.

          Is this suitable for beginners?

          Yes. It begins with virtualization and Kali Linux setup before introducing Cybersecurity topics.

          Do I need previous experience?

          No. Basic computer knowledge is sufficient; networking knowledge is helpful.

          Is this an official CEH course?

          No. It is independent HackStark education and does not award EC Council certification.

          Are TechFly and Oxege Technologies owned by HackStark?

          No. They are independent training partners. Neither Muhammad Ahmed Talha nor HackStark owns either organization.

          Does it include practical demonstrations?

          Yes. Lab demonstrations must only be reproduced in controlled, authorized environments.

          Is all content current?

          Foundational and legacy lessons are clearly labeled, with current official documentation linked where available.

          Can I practice on public websites?

          No. Use your own systems, intentionally vulnerable labs, CTFs or explicitly authorized targets only.

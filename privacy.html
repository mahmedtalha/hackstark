Privacy

Last updated — September 7, 2026

A lightweight, privacy-minded website

HackStark does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, account sign-in or a contact-form database on this website. You can browse the primary content without sharing personal information with HackStark.

Local preferences

If you change the color theme, the website stores that preference in your browser using local storage. The preference stays on your device and does not contain sensitive information.

External services

The projects section may request public repository metadata from GitHub. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy practices apply. Static fallback content remains available if these requests are blocked.

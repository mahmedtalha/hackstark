Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated — September 12, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HackStark ("HackStark," "we," "us" or "our") handles information when you visit this website, use HackStark Academy, create an account or contact us. You can browse most public content without creating an account.

We do not currently sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers. If our practices change, we will update this policy.

Information we collect

Account information. If you sign up or sign in, our authentication provider, Clerk, processes the details you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, profile image and authentication credentials. HackStark may receive account identifiers and profile details needed to recognize your account and operate signed-in features. Passwords and third-party sign-in credentials are handled by Clerk and the relevant identity provider.

Communications. If you email us, submit a form or report a security issue, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details and message.

Technical information. Our hosting and authentication providers may process information such as IP address, browser and device type, request timestamps, cookie identifiers and security events to deliver and protect the service.

Local storage and cookies

HackStark stores theme preferences and course progress in your browser's local storage. Course progress records lesson states such as Not started, In progress or Completed. JARVIS conversations run locally in your browser and are not sent to an AI service by this website. You can remove these items with the site's reset controls or by clearing browser data.

Clerk uses cookies and similar browser storage that are necessary to create, maintain and secure authentication sessions. Blocking those technologies may prevent account features from working.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure account features, operate the website and Academy, remember your settings, respond to questions and security reports, diagnose problems, prevent misuse, comply with law and improve our educational resources.

Service providers and external services

We share information only as needed with providers that help us operate the service, when you direct us to do so, or when required for safety or legal compliance. Clerk provides authentication and user management. Its processing of HackStark account data is governed by our arrangements with Clerk and this policy; Clerk's own website activities are described in its privacy policy.

The projects section may request public repository information from GitHub. YouTube's image host supplies video thumbnails when they enter the page. Links may take you to GitHub, YouTube, Google Forms and social platforms. Those services process information under their own policies once you interact with them.

Retention and security

We retain account and communication information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including security, record-keeping and legal obligations. Browser-local preferences remain until you reset them or clear your browser data. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may update certain account details through your profile controls and can sign out at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or an objection to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing it.

Children's privacy

HackStark is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to the processing of their information in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required permission, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

International use

HackStark and its providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, providers use recognized safeguards for international data transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, its providers or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here and update the date above. Material changes may also be highlighted on the website when appropriate.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email hackstarkofficial@gmail.com.

Suggested learning path

From first lab to defensive practice.

The broader historical curriculum is organized here as a safe, modern roadmap. Every practical exercise belongs in an isolated environment or on systems you are explicitly authorized to assess.

  1. 01
    Prepare the lab

    Virtualization, Kali Linux setup, repositories, networking basics and ethical-hacking foundations.

    Understand exposure

    Footprinting, lawful OSINT, network scanning, enumeration and vulnerability analysis.

  3. 03
    Strengthen defenses

  4. 04
    Explore specializations

Course overview

A structured path into cybersecurity.

Ethical Hacking Course for Beginners by HackStark combines 46 self-paced lessons with practical demonstrations and supporting resources.

Start with an isolated Kali Linux lab, then progress through reconnaissance, vulnerability assessment, systems, networks, web applications, wireless security, IoT, cloud computing and cryptography.

Independent education

The curriculum covers topics historically aligned with CEH v11-era domains. It is not official or authorized EC-Council certification training, and completion does not earn CEH certification.

Course questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. It begins with virtualization and Kali Linux setup before introducing cybersecurity topics.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Basic computer knowledge is sufficient; networking knowledge is helpful.

Is this an official CEH course?

No. It is independent HackStark education and does not award EC-Council certification.

Does it include practical demonstrations?

Yes. Lab demonstrations must only be reproduced in controlled, authorized environments.

Is all content current?

Foundational and legacy lessons are clearly labeled, with current official documentation linked where available.

Can I practice on public websites?

No. Use your own systems, intentionally vulnerable labs, CTFs or explicitly authorized targets only.

Where is my course progress stored?

Progress is stored only in your browser on this device. HackStark does not receive it, and you can reset it from the curriculum.

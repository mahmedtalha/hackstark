Responsible use

Terms

Last updated — September 8, 2026

Educational purpose

HackStark publishes content and open-source resources for cybersecurity education, defensive research and authorized security testing. The material is intended to help learners understand systems, vulnerabilities and defensive practices.

Authorization is required

Only use security techniques on systems, networks, applications and devices you own or have explicit permission to assess. Do not use HackStark material for unauthorized access, disruption, data theft, credential theft or malicious activity.

Practice safely

Potentially disruptive concepts should be explored only in isolated, controlled laboratory environments. You are responsible for understanding and following the laws, policies and scope requirements that apply to your work.

External resources

This website links to third-party platforms such as GitHub and YouTube. Their terms and availability are managed by those services. Open-source repositories may include their own licenses and documentation.
